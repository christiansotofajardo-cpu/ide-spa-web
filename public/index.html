FEATURED INITIATIVE / EARLY-STAGE PROTOTYPE

CLARA Salud

A bilingual educational prototype exploring a critical challenge in sexual and reproductive health communication: accessing information is not the same as understanding it.

The current prototype offers Spanish and English information retrieval and experimental teach-back activities. Planned work includes clinically reviewed knowledge resources, better contextual language processing, accessible voice interaction and rigorous safety and usability evaluation.

CLARA Salud is an educational prototype. Its comprehension scores are heuristic; the content and system are not clinically validated, and it does not replace professional medical care.

FEATURED INITIATIVE / OPEN-SOURCE PROTOTYPE

ESCUDO Digital

ESCUDO Digital is a bilingual, privacy-conscious web prototype developed to help girls and adolescents recognize possible warning signs in digital conversations.

The platform combines interpretable linguistic rules, experimental machine learning and discourse-pattern analysis to identify possible threats, blackmail, emotional pressure, coercive requests and attempts to obtain sensitive personal information.

Users voluntarily submit messages for analysis. Processing occurs within their own browser, without automatic social media monitoring or transmission of conversations to an analysis server.

ESCUDO provides understandable explanations of detected signals, basic protective guidance, and fictional learning activities designed to encourage safer decisions and access to human support.

ESCUDO Digital is an experimental educational and digital safety prototype. Its detection performance has not been independently validated. The system may miss warning signs or generate false alerts. It does not replace qualified professional advice or emergency services.
